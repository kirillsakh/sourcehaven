Two voices, two typefacesThe app speaks in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, designed by the Braille Institute for maximum legibility. Quotes from your documents are set in Source Serif 4, so evidence visibly reads as "from the document".
One bold thing: the highlighterCitations are ink-blue tabs; pointing at or clicking one sweeps the highlighter across its quote. Yellow is used for nothing else. (The mockup marks a phrase; the API only knows the whole quote, so the real app highlights the whole quote.)
Everything else stays quietNo cards, shadows or gradients. Lists are separated by rules, not boxes. Labels are sentence case. Radius varies by role: composer 12, tray 10, buttons 8, tabs 4.
Readable by default16.5 px answers, 15 px interface, 13.5 px minimum, answers capped at 64 characters per line. Both fonts are free (SIL OFL) and bundled with the app.
